## ✂️ How to Use HyperSlice

1. **Open HyperSlice**: Once installed, launch the application from your computer’s start menu or applications folder.
2. **Load Your Media**: Click on the "Load Media" button to select the file you want to work with.
3. **Select Elements**: Use the selection tool to outline the area you want to cut. The AI will assist in identifying edges for a smoother cut.
4. **Preview Your Cut**: Check the preview to see how your selection looks.
5. **Export the File**: Click on the “Export” button to save your cut in your preferred format.
